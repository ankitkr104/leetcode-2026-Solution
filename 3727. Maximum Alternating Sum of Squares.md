# Problem Statement

You are given an integer array `nums`. You may rearrange its elements in any order.

The alternating score of an array `arr` is defined as:

    score = arr[0]² - arr[1]² + arr[2]² - arr[3]² + ...

Return the maximum possible alternating score after rearranging the elements of `nums`.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input:**
    nums = [1,2,3]

**Output:**
    12

**Explanation:**

One optimal rearrangement is `[2,1,3]`.

    score = 2² - 1² + 3²
          = 4 - 1 + 9
          = 12

### Example 2

**Input:**
    nums = [1,-1,2,-2,3,-3]

**Output:**
    16

**Explanation:**

One optimal rearrangement is `[-3,-1,-2,1,3,2]`.

    score = (-3)² - (-1)² + (-2)² - 1² + 3² - 2²
          = 9 - 1 + 4 - 1 + 9 - 4
          = 16

## Constraints

- `1 <= nums.length <= 10^5`
- `-4 * 10^4 <= nums[i] <= 4 * 10^4`
