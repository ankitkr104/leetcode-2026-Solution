# Problem Statement

Alice and Bob are traveling to Rome for separate business meetings.

You are given four strings:

- `arriveAlice`: Alice's arrival date.
- `leaveAlice`: Alice's departure date.
- `arriveBob`: Bob's arrival date.
- `leaveBob`: Bob's departure date.

Each date is represented as a five-character string in the format `"MM-DD"`.

Alice stays in Rome from `arriveAlice` to `leaveAlice`, inclusive. Bob stays from `arriveBob` to `leaveBob`, inclusive.

Return the total number of days Alice and Bob spend in Rome together.

All dates belong to the same non-leap year.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input:**
    arriveAlice = "08-15"
    leaveAlice = "08-18"
    arriveBob = "08-16"
    leaveBob = "08-19"

**Output:**
    3

**Explanation:**

Alice stays from August 15 to August 18, while Bob stays from August 16 to August 19.

They are together on August 16, 17, and 18.

### Example 2

**Input:**
    arriveAlice = "10-01"
    leaveAlice = "10-31"
    arriveBob = "11-01"
    leaveBob = "12-31"

**Output:**
    0

**Explanation:**

Their stays do not overlap, so the answer is `0`.

## Constraints

- All dates use the format `"MM-DD"`.
- Each arrival date is earlier than or equal to its corresponding departure date.
- All dates are valid dates in the same non-leap year.
