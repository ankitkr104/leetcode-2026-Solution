# Problem Statement

You are given two integers `n` and `t`.

Return the **smallest integer greater than or equal to `n`** such that the **product of its digits** is divisible by `t`.

---

## Example 1

```text
Input

n = 10

t = 2
```

Output

```text
10
```

### Explanation

Digit product of `10` is

```text
1 × 0 = 0
```

Since

```text
0 % 2 = 0
```

the answer is

```text
10
```

---

## Example 2

```text
Input

n = 15

t = 3
```

Output

```text
16
```

### Explanation

Digit product of `15`

```text
1 × 5 = 5
```

Not divisible by

```text
3
```

Digit product of `16`

```text
1 × 6 = 6
```

Since

```text
6 % 3 = 0
```

the answer is

```text
16
```

---

## Constraints

```text
1 <= n <= 100

1 <= t <= 10
```
