# Problem Statement

You are given a 0-indexed integer array `nums`.

The distinct count of a subarray `nums[i..j]` is the number of unique elements in that subarray.

Return the sum of the squares of the distinct counts of all non-empty contiguous subarrays of `nums`.

Since the answer may be very large, return it modulo `10^9 + 7`.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input:**
    nums = [1,2,1]

**Output:**
    15

**Explanation:**

The six possible subarrays are:

| Subarray | Distinct Count | Square |
|---|---:|---:|
| [1] | 1 | 1 |
| [2] | 1 | 1 |
| [1] | 1 | 1 |
| [1,2] | 2 | 4 |
| [2,1] | 2 | 4 |
| [1,2,1] | 2 | 4 |

Total:

    1 + 1 + 1 + 4 + 4 + 4 = 15

### Example 2

**Input:**
    nums = [2,2]

**Output:**
    3

**Explanation:**

The three subarrays are `[2]`, `[2]`, and `[2,2]`. Each has one distinct element.

    1² + 1² + 1² = 3

## Constraints

- `1 <= nums.length <= 10^5`
- `1 <= nums[i] <= 10^5`
