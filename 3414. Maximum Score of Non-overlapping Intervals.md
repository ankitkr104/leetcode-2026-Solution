## Problem Statement

You are given a 2D integer array `intervals`, where:

    intervals[i] = [li, ri, weighti]

Interval `i` starts at position `li` and ends at position `ri`, with weight `weighti`.

You can choose **at most 4 non-overlapping intervals**.

The score of the chosen intervals is the sum of their weights.

Return the **lexicographically smallest array of at most 4 indices** from `intervals` that has the maximum possible score.

### Important

Two intervals are considered non-overlapping only if they **do not share any points**.

Therefore, if:

    interval A ends at x
    interval B starts at x

they are considered **overlapping**.

So for two intervals:

    [l1, r1]
    [l2, r2]

they are non-overlapping when:

    r1 < l2

---

## Examples

### Example 1

Input:

    intervals = [
        [1,3,2],
        [4,5,2],
        [1,5,5],
        [6,9,3],
        [6,7,1],
        [8,9,1]
    ]

Output:

    [2,3]

Explanation:

Choose intervals:

    index 2 -> [1,5,5]
    index 3 -> [6,9,3]

They do not overlap because:

    5 < 6

Their total score is:

    5 + 3 = 8

This is the maximum possible score.

---

### Example 2

Input:

    intervals = [
        [5,8,1],
        [6,7,7],
        [4,7,3],
        [9,10,6],
        [7,8,2],
        [11,14,3],
        [3,5,5]
    ]

Output:

    [1,3,5,6]

Explanation:

Choose:

    index 1 -> [6,7,7]
    index 3 -> [9,10,6]
    index 5 -> [11,14,3]
    index 6 -> [3,5,5]

Total score:

    7 + 6 + 3 + 5 = 21

These four intervals are non-overlapping.

---

## Constraints

- `1 <= intervals.length <= 5 * 10^4`
- `intervals[i].length == 3`
- `1 <= li <= ri <= 10^9`
- `1 <= weighti <= 10^9`
