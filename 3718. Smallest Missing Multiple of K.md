## Problem Statement

Given an integer array `nums` and an integer `k`, return the **smallest positive multiple** of `k` that is **missing** from `nums`.

A multiple of `k` is any positive integer that is divisible by `k`.

In other words, we need to find the smallest value among:

    k, 2k, 3k, 4k, ...

that does not exist in `nums`.

---

## Example 1

**Input:**

    nums = [8,2,3,4,6]
    k = 2

**Output:**

    10

### Explanation

The positive multiples of `2` are:

    2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, ...

Check them one by one:

    2 -> present
    4 -> present
    6 -> present
    8 -> present
    10 -> missing

Therefore:

    answer = 10

---

## Example 2

**Input:**

    nums = [1,4,7,10,15]
    k = 5

**Output:**

    5

### Explanation

The positive multiples of `5` are:

    5, 10, 15, 20, ...

Check:

    5 -> missing

Therefore, the smallest missing multiple is:

    5

---

## Constraints

- `1 <= nums.length <= 100`
- `1 <= nums[i] <= 100`
- `1 <= k <= 100`
