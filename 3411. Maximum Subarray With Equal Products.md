# Problem Statement
You are given an array of positive integers `nums`.

A subarray is called **product equivalent** if:

`product(subarray) == gcd(subarray) * lcm(subarray)`

Where:
- `product` is the product of all elements.
- `gcd` is the greatest common divisor.
- `lcm` is the least common multiple.

Return the length of the longest product-equivalent subarray.

## Examples

**Example 1:**
- Input: `nums = [1,2,1,2,1,1,1]`
- Output: `5`
- Explanation: The subarray `[1,2,1,1,1]` has product `2`, GCD `1`, and LCM `2`.

**Example 2:**
- Input: `nums = [2,3,4,5,6]`
- Output: `3`
- Explanation: The subarray `[3,4,5]` has product `60`, GCD `1`, and LCM `60`.

**Example 3:**
- Input: `nums = [1,2,3,1,4,5,1]`
- Output: `5`

## Constraints
- `2 <= nums.length <= 100`
- `1 <= nums[i] <= 10`
